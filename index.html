From curriculum to teacher talk to student knowledge

A three-graph Bayesian model of semantic learning. The curriculum defines opportunities to learn, teacher talk instantiates a subset of those relations, and student knowledge remains latent until evidence updates our belief about each relation.

What is empirical vs. proposed? Relation examples and the teacher-network framework come from the READS materials. The edge-level Bayesian student model and numeric demo strengths below are proposed / synthetic.

Teacher enactment is an exposure process

The teacher-talk work treats each utterance as a typed semantic link. Repetition becomes edge weight; breadth and structure come from the network as a whole.

This demo treats more mentions as stronger exposure, but it does not assume repetition is always better. The READS materials explicitly leave open whether repeated links strengthen learning or crowd out relational variety.
