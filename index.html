Model notes
What is source-derived?
The curriculum-network pipeline, teacher semantic triples, relation taxonomy, and the conceptual dimensions of rich teacher networks come from the READS materials you provided.
What is proposed here?
The latent student edge graph, the contextual Beta prior, fractional evidence updates, and the direct coupling from teacher enactment to student-edge probability are modeling proposals to test.
Where does ConceptNet fit?
As an optional external relational prior or validation source. It should complement curriculum-derived distributional structure, not replace classroom evidence.
